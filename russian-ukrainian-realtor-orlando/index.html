Who We Are

We are Vlad Kizlo and Anastasiia Zbihla, a husband and wife Realtor team at Keller Williams Realty At The Lakes. We live in Windermere, we have closed more than one hundred homes since 2020, and we hold 4.9 stars across 120 Google reviews. We also happen to speak your language.

That last part matters more than it sounds. An American real estate transaction is a stack of documents most native speakers never fully read: the purchase contract, the inspection report, the HOA disclosures, the insurance binder, the closing statement. We walk through each of them with you in plain language, yours, before you sign. Not a summary, not "do not worry, it is standard," but what the paper actually obligates you to.

Common Questions

What families ask us.

Do you help Ukrainian families relocating to Orlando?

Yes, this is a large part of our work. Many of our clients arrived in Florida after 2022 and are building a life here from the beginning, as our own family did. We help with the home search and the purchase itself, and with what surrounds it: the contract, the inspection, HOA rules and Florida insurance explained in Ukrainian, plus lenders, schools and the practical contacts a new family needs.

Can we do the entire purchase in Russian or Ukrainian?

The conversation with us, yes, entirely. The documents themselves are in English, as Florida law and the lenders require, but we walk through every page with you in your language before you sign, so you know exactly what each paper obligates you to. Nothing gets signed on a summary or a reassurance.

Which areas of Orlando do you serve?

Our home market is West Orlando: Windermere, where we live, Winter Garden, and Horizon West with its new construction around Hamlin. We also work Dr. Phillips, Clermont and the greater Orlando area wherever our clients' work and schools take them.

We are not in Florida yet. Can we start from abroad?

Yes. Much of the early work, narrowing areas, understanding budgets, watching the market, happens well before you land. We do video walkthroughs of homes, explain each neighborhood honestly, and time the serious steps for when you are here. The first call costs nothing and can happen in whichever language you think in.

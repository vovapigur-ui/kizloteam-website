Neighborhood Guides

West Orlando, known house by house.

We live here, we invest here, and we wrote these guides the way we brief our own buyers: real prices, real tradeoffs, no brochure language.

Windermere

01 · Lakes, estates, A-rated schools

The Butler Chain of Lakes, guard gated golf communities like Isleworth and Keene's Pointe, and a small town center that still feels like old Florida. Median around $714K, estates well past $5M.

Winter Garden

02 · Plant Street, trail life, charm

Historic downtown, the golf cart district, the West Orange Trail and a farmers market people drive across the metro for. Median around $560K, premiums near downtown.

Horizon West

03 · New construction, Hamlin, growth

One of the fastest growing master planned areas in the country. New schools, Hamlin Town Center, and builder inventory that rewards represented buyers. Median around $590K.

Also Served

Beyond the big three

Celebration, Dr. Phillips, Clermont, Ocoee, Oakland Park and greater Orlando. If you are weighing one of these against Windermere or Winter Garden, ask us for the honest comparison.

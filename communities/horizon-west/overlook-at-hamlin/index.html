Living in Overlook at Hamlin

Overlook at Hamlin and Overlook 2 at Hamlin share the west shore of Lake Hancock in the 34787 zip, about four minutes by car from Hamlin Town Center. The association clubhouse is at 7709 Lake Hancock Boulevard.

Taylor Morrison is the builder named on the MLS listings. These are bigger homes than most of Horizon West: the median sale this year was about 2,900 square feet, and about half the homes sold had a private pool.

Why buyers pick it: the lake, the size, and Hamlin Town Center close by, without a CDD on the county tax roll. The tradeoff is price. The median here runs well above the Horizon West average.

How it compares with Summerlake across the lake and the newer villages is in our Horizon West guide, and the town center is covered in our Hamlin guide.

Overlook at Hamlin HOA and CDD

Short answer: HOA yes, and no CDD. The 2026 Orange County tax roll shows no CDD assessment on the parcels we checked, and MLS shows “CDD: No” on every closed sale in the last twelve months.

  1. HOA dues by section

    MLS lists dues of about $210 to $340 a month on most of this year’s sales, depending on the section. The lakefront estates on John Hancock Drive list about $1,650 to $1,680 a year. Amenities listed on MLS include the clubhouse, fitness center, pool, playground and dog park.

  2. No Community Development District

    We checked three parcels across Overlook and Overlook 2, including a John Hancock Drive lakefront home on the Orange County Property Appraiser’s 2026 record: no CDD line, only the county’s standard garbage, stormwater and street light charges of about $650 to $710 a year. All 29 closed sales in the last twelve months also show no CDD in Stellar MLS. We still confirm it on the specific home before you write an offer.

  3. Lake Hancock

    The community runs along the west shore of Lake Hancock, the same lake Summerlake and Hamlin Town Center share. Ask us which homes have true lake frontage and which only have a view; it is the biggest price driver here.

Buying or selling in Overlook at Hamlin

What we tell buyers before they tour, and sellers before they list.

  1. Lakefront, lake view or interior

    Sales here ran from $487,000 to $2.65 million in one year. Lake frontage, lot and pool drive most of that spread, so compare a home only with others in the same position.

  2. Pools change the comps

    Half of this year’s sales had a private pool. A house without one should be priced against the other half, not the median.

  3. Sellers: the market is moving

    At 25 median days on market, well priced homes still go quickly here. We bring the lakefront and interior comps separately to every listing appointment.

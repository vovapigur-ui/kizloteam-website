What Horizon West offers

Horizon West is not one neighborhood. It is a 1990s Orange County master plan that laid out villages around a town center, each with its own schools, parks and price band, then let builders fill them in over twenty five years.

Hamlin holds the town center. Summerlake and Independence are the established side. Waterleigh, Lakeside Village and Village I are where the cranes still are.

What you get: new and nearly new homes, new schools, resort style amenity centers and prices below Windermere’s, with the 429 running to Disney, the hospitals and downtown.

The tradeoffs: young trees instead of mature oaks, an HOA on every street, and construction nearby for the next few years. For oaks and older streets, look at downtown Winter Garden or the established side of Windermere.

Resale sellers here compete with builders who are answering 7.4 percent mortgage rates with buydowns. The full picture is in our October 2026 market report. Buying to rent instead? See how we underwrite a Central Florida investment property.

HOA and CDD fees in Horizon West

Two similar Horizon West homes can carry monthly costs that differ by several hundred dollars, and the list price will not tell you. We pull the real figures for every home we show: HOA dues from the association, the CDD line from the Orange County Property Appraiser, and the bond payoff year from the district, the same day you ask.

How HOA, CDD and incentives work

  • HOA dues are universal, and they vary

    Every Horizon West village has a homeowners association. Dues generally run from about $100 to $250 a month depending on the amenity package, with townhome communities on the higher end because exterior maintenance is included.

  • CDD assessments depend on the phase

    A Community Development District is a bond that paid for the roads, drainage and utilities before the first home sold, repaid through your property tax bill. Newer phases in Lakeside Village, Village I and parts of Waterleigh typically carry $1,500 to $3,500 a year. Older villages can be lighter: Summerlake parcels we checked for tax year 2026 showed no CDD line at all, only the county’s standard stormwater, garbage and street light assessments of about $657 a year.

  • Builder incentives change the real payment

    At 7.4 percent, a builder’s rate buydown is worth more than a price cut of the same size. We compare the effective monthly payment on a new build against a three year old resale with the upgrades already done, because that is the number you live with, not the sticker.

Schools and drive times

Orange County Public Schools has opened a new Horizon West campus almost every year to keep up with growth, and each opening redraws the lines. These are the 2026 to 2027 assignments we see most often. Confirm any specific address with the OCPS school locator before you write an offer.

The 429 is the reason Horizon West exists. Drive times are free flow from Summerlake, the geographic middle of the villages. Add ten to fifteen minutes at 8 a.m.

Horizon West questions

The questions relocating buyers ask us most. Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

Is Horizon West a good place to live?

It is one of the fastest growing master planned areas in the country, built as connected villages with new schools, parks and Hamlin Town Center, with Disney a short drive down the 429. The honest tradeoffs are construction activity, growing traffic, an HOA everywhere and CDD assessments in the newer phases.

How much does a home in Horizon West cost in 2026?

The August 2026 median sale price was $590,000 across all of Horizon West, flat year over year. By village, third quarter closed medians ran from about $553,000 in Waterleigh and $555,000 in Lakeside Village to about $671,000 in Hamlin and $696,000 in Village I. Townhomes closed around $408,000. Builder pricing and incentives move month to month, which is one of the main reasons to bring your own representation.

Which Horizon West villages have CDD fees?

Most of the newer phases do: Lakeside Village, Village I and parts of Waterleigh typically carry $1,500 to $3,500 a year on the tax bill. Older villages can be lighter. Summerlake parcels we checked for tax year 2026 showed no CDD line at all. The only way to know for a specific home is to read the property appraiser record, which we do for every address we show.

What schools serve Horizon West?

Horizon High and Windermere High at the top, Hamlin Middle, Horizon West Middle and Water Spring Middle in between, and a growing list of elementaries including Summerlake, Hamlin, Independence, Water Spring and Panther Lake. Orange County opens a new campus here almost every year and redraws the zones when it does, so confirm any address with the OCPS school locator before you write an offer.

What is Horizon West’s town center?

Hamlin Town Center on Lake Hancock is Horizon West’s retail and dining hub: Publix and Walmart for the errands, the Cinépolis dine-in cinema, waterfront restaurants and the new Horizon West library branch. It is the closest thing the area has to a downtown, and proximity to it is showing up in resale values. More in our Hamlin guide.

Should I buy new construction or resale in Horizon West?

New construction gets you warranties and incentives, but often base pricing plus expensive upgrades and a wait. Resale gets you completed upgrades, fenced yards and established streets, often at a better effective price. With 40 percent of Horizon West listings cutting price in 2026 and builders offering rate buydowns, we run this comparison fresh for every buyer, and the answer changes with each builder’s incentive package. The town center village has both side by side; see our Hamlin guide.

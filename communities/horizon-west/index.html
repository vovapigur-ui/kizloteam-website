Horizon West, Florida.

Six planned villages in southwest Orange County, a town center on Lake Hancock, and the 429 running down the middle. This is where most of West Orlando's new construction lives, and where the fee structure, the school zone and the builder's incentive matter as much as the price.

Median $590K · 42 days on market · 40% of listings cut price · Updated October 2026

The Short Version

Horizon West is not one neighborhood. It is a 1990s Orange County master plan that laid out villages around a town center, each with its own schools, parks and price band, and then let builders fill them in over twenty five years. Hamlin holds the town center. Summerlake and Independence are the established side. Waterleigh, Lakeside Village and Village I are where the cranes still are.

Who it fits: buyers who want a new or nearly new home, new schools and resort amenities without Windermere pricing, and who commute on the 429 to Disney, the hospitals or downtown. Who it does not fit: buyers who want mature oaks, no HOA, or quiet from construction for the next few years. For that, look at downtown Winter Garden or the older streets of Windermere.

The market right now: Redfin's August 2026 median sale price for Horizon West was $590,000, flat year over year, on 386 closings. Homes took 42 median days to sell at 97.8 percent of list, and 40 percent of listings cut their price before selling, the highest share of any West Orlando market. Resale sellers here compete with builders, and the builders are answering 7.4 percent mortgage rates with buydowns. The full picture is in our October 2026 market report.

The Monthly Cost

HOA and CDD, explained.

Two similar Horizon West homes can carry monthly costs that differ by several hundred dollars, and the list price will not tell you. This is the part relocating buyers get wrong most often.

  1. HOA dues are universal, and they vary

    Every Horizon West village has a homeowners association. Dues generally run from about $100 to $250 a month depending on the amenity package, with townhome communities on the higher end because exterior maintenance is included. The community pool, fitness center and gates are what you are paying for.

  2. CDD assessments depend on the phase

    A Community Development District is a bond that paid for the roads, drainage and utilities before the first home sold, repaid through your property tax bill. Newer phases in Lakeside Village, Village I and parts of Waterleigh typically carry $1,500 to $3,500 a year. Older villages can be lighter: Summerlake parcels we checked for tax year 2026 showed no CDD line at all, only the county's standard stormwater, garbage and street light assessments of about $657 a year.

  3. Builder incentives change the real payment

    At 7.4 percent, a builder's rate buydown is worth more than a price cut of the same size. We compare the effective monthly payment on a new build against a three year old resale with the upgrades already done, because that is the number you live with, not the sticker.

  4. We pull the actual figures for every home we show

    HOA dues from the association, the CDD line from the Orange County Property Appraiser, the bond balance and payoff year from the district. Ask us for the sheet on any address and you will have it the same day.

Horizon West Questions

What buyers ask us.

Is Horizon West a good place to live?

It is one of the fastest growing master planned areas in the country, built as connected villages with new schools, parks and Hamlin Town Center. It fits buyers who want new construction and Disney proximity via the 429. The honest tradeoffs are construction activity, growing traffic, HOA structure everywhere and CDD assessments in the newer phases.

How much does a home in Horizon West cost in 2026?

The August 2026 median sale price was $590,000 across all of Horizon West, flat year over year. By village, third quarter closed medians ran from about $553,000 in Waterleigh and $555,000 in Lakeside Village to about $671,000 in Hamlin and $696,000 in Village I. Townhomes closed around $408,000. Builder pricing and incentives move month to month, which is one of the main reasons to bring your own representation.

Which Horizon West villages have CDD fees?

Most of the newer phases do: Lakeside Village, Village I and parts of Waterleigh typically carry $1,500 to $3,500 a year on the tax bill. Older villages can be lighter. Summerlake parcels we checked for tax year 2026 showed no CDD line at all. The only way to know for a specific home is to read the property appraiser record, which we do for every address we show.

What schools serve Horizon West?

Horizon High and Windermere High at the top, Hamlin Middle, Horizon West Middle and Water Spring Middle in between, and a growing list of elementaries including Summerlake, Hamlin, Independence, Water Spring and Panther Lake. Orange County opens a new campus here almost every year and redraws the zones when it does, so confirm any address with the OCPS school locator before you write an offer.

What is Hamlin Town Center?

Horizon West's retail and dining hub on Lake Hancock: Publix and Walmart for the errands, the Cinépolis dine-in cinema, waterfront restaurants and the new Horizon West library branch. It is the closest thing the area has to a downtown, and proximity to it is showing up in resale values.

New construction or resale?

New construction gets you warranties and incentives, but often base pricing plus expensive upgrades and a wait. Resale gets you completed upgrades, fenced yards and established streets, often at a better effective price. With 40 percent of Horizon West listings cutting price in 2026 and builders offering rate buydowns, we run this comparison fresh for every buyer, and the answer changes with each builder's incentive package.

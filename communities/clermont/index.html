Clermont, Florida.

Hills, a chain of lakes you can actually boat across, and a downtown that stayed a downtown. Clermont is the part of Central Florida that does not look like the rest of it.

Lake County · Chain of Lakes · Ask us for this week's numbers

The Short Version

Clermont sits on the Lake Wales Ridge, which is why it has hills in a state famous for not having any. That elevation, the Chain of Lakes and a genuine historic downtown are the three things people move here for.

Who it fits: buyers who want more land and more elevation than West Orlando gives them, families who will use the South Lake Trail and the water, and anyone who would rather drive a bit further than pay Windermere prices. Who it does not fit: buyers who need to be fifteen minutes from Disney or downtown Orlando. That is what Horizon West and Windermere are for.

Inside Clermont

What changes the price here.

Clermont is less uniform than a master planned community. Two houses a street apart can price very differently, and it usually comes down to these.

WaterLakefront on the Chain, lake access through a community ramp, and lake view with no access are three different prices. They get advertised as if they were one.

Lifestyle

The lake, the trail, and the hills.

Waterfront Park sits on Lake Minneola in the middle of town, with the South Lake Trail running along the water and on toward Winter Garden. The National Training Center draws endurance athletes here year round, which is why you will see more cyclists on these roads than anywhere else in Central Florida. Downtown, Montrose Street keeps the small brick-and-awning scale that most Florida towns traded away.

The Citrus Tower has stood on the ridge since 1956, back when the view was groves in every direction. Most of those groves are neighbourhoods now, which is the short history of why Clermont grew.

Clermont Questions

What buyers ask us.

  1. Why is Clermont hillier than the rest of Florida?

    Clermont sits on the Lake Wales Ridge, an ancient sand ridge that runs down the middle of the peninsula. It gives the city genuine elevation change, which is why you see long climbs on the trail and why so many homes here have a view worth paying for. It also means lots and drainage vary more street to street than they do out on the flat, so a walk of the property matters.

  2. How far is Clermont from Orlando?

    Roughly 25 miles from downtown Orlando, mostly on SR 50 or the Turnpike. Plenty of our Clermont clients commute toward Orlando or Winter Garden, and the trade is straightforward: more land and more elevation for your money, in exchange for a longer drive than you would have from Horizon West.

  3. What is the Clermont Chain of Lakes?

    A connected run of lakes, with Lake Minneola the one most people picture, that you can boat across without trailering. Waterfront Park sits on Minneola in the middle of town, and the South Lake Trail runs along it. Lakefront and lake access pricing here works differently from inland streets, and we will show you which is which before you make an offer.

  4. Do you actually work Clermont, or just Windermere?

    We work it. Clermont, Minneola and Groveland are part of our regular market alongside Windermere and Winter Garden, and we live close enough to be there the same day. If a listing needs eyes on it this afternoon, that is a phone call, not a scheduling problem.

Winter Garden, Florida.

A real downtown, a farmers market people cross the metro for, and streets where golf carts outnumber second cars on Saturdays. Winter Garden earned its premium.

Median $560K · ~48 days on market · Updated August 2026

The Short Version

Plant Street is the spine: the Saturday farmers market, Plant Street Market's twenty plus vendors, Crooked Can Brewing in the middle, and the West Orange Trail running straight through downtown.

Who it fits: buyers who want character and community over gate codes, families who will actually use the trail, and anyone priced out of Windermere who refuses to feel like they settled. Who it does not fit: buyers who need brand new construction on every corner. That is what Horizon West is for.

Lifestyle

Saturdays on Plant Street.

The West Orange Trail runs 22 miles through downtown, the farmers market fills the pavilion every Saturday, and the brewery crowd spills onto the sidewalk by late afternoon. It is the kind of downtown other suburbs try to build from scratch and cannot.

Buyers notice within one visit. It is usually the moment they stop asking about other cities.

Winter Garden Questions

What buyers ask us.

Is Winter Garden a good place to live?

It pairs a genuinely walkable historic downtown with newer family neighborhoods and direct trail access. The Saturday farmers market, Plant Street Market and Crooked Can give it character most Orlando suburbs never develop. Tradeoffs: downtown premiums are real, and traffic on the 429 corridor grows every year.

How much does a home here cost in 2026?

The citywide median is around $560,000, with neighborhood medians running roughly $540,000 to $730,000. Homes inside the golf cart district and near Plant Street carry documented premiums and rarely last long when priced correctly.

What is the golf cart district?

The zone around historic downtown where registered golf carts can legally drive on the streets. Living inside it means the farmers market and dinner on Plant Street without touching a car, which is exactly why those blocks command a premium.

Winter Garden or Horizon West?

Choose historic Winter Garden for character, mature trees and walkability. Choose Horizon West for new construction, new schools and master planned amenities. Prices overlap heavily, so the decision is about lifestyle, and we help buyers weigh it almost every week.

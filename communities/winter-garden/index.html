Living in Winter Garden

Plant Street is the spine: the Saturday farmers market, Plant Street Market’s twenty plus vendors, Crooked Can Brewing in the middle, and the West Orange Trail running straight through downtown.

What it offers: character and community over gate codes, a downtown you can reach by trail or golf cart, and streets from the historic district to 1990s and 2010s builds on larger lots, at a typical value below Windermere’s.

What it does not offer: brand new construction on every corner. That is what Horizon West is for.

Neighborhoods and prices

Redfin’s median sale price for Winter Garden over the three months ending September 2026 was $599,714, up 7.1 percent from a year earlier, with 172 homes sold in September. Homes took 54 median days to sell, up from 45, and sold at 97.7 percent of list, with 40.5 percent of listings taking a price cut. Zillow’s typical value tells a flatter story at $568,910, down 0.5 percent, with homes pending in about 41 days.

Three zones explain most of the price differences in this city.

Is Winter Garden a good place to live?

It pairs a genuinely walkable historic downtown with newer neighborhoods and direct trail access. The Saturday farmers market, Plant Street Market and Crooked Can give it character most Orlando suburbs never develop. Tradeoffs: downtown premiums are real, and traffic on the 429 corridor grows every year.

How much does a home in Winter Garden cost in 2026?

Zillow’s typical value is about $567,000; Redfin’s August median sale price was $599,054. Neighborhood figures run roughly $540,000 to $730,000, and homes inside the golf cart district and near Plant Street carry a premium and rarely last long when priced correctly.

What is the golf cart district?

The zone around historic downtown where registered golf carts can legally drive on the streets. Living inside it means the farmers market and dinner on Plant Street without touching a car, which is exactly why those blocks command a premium.

Winter Garden or Horizon West?

Choose historic Winter Garden for character, mature trees and walkability. Choose Horizon West for new construction, new schools and master planned amenities. Prices overlap heavily, so the decision is about lifestyle, and we help buyers weigh it almost every week. For the three-way view with Windermere, explore our communities.

Keene's Pointe, Windermere.

Fourteen villages, a Jack Nicklaus course at the center, and the Butler Chain at the edges. Keene's Pointe is where Windermere's gated life comes with children on bicycles.

Guard gated · ~1,000 homes · 14 villages · Updated August 2026

The Short Version

Keene's Pointe is a staffed, gated community of roughly a thousand custom homes arranged into fourteen villages around The Golden Bear Club and its Jack Nicklaus Signature golf course, with frontage and resident access on the Butler Chain of Lakes.

If Isleworth is Windermere's old guard, Keene's Pointe is its family seat. The lots are generous, the architecture is custom without being theatrical, and the community actually gets used: golf carts to the club, kayaks on the interior lakes, school runs to the A-rated zone that anchors this side of town. The club is the social center for members, but the parks, trails and playgrounds mean the community works for non-members too.

The wider context, the Butler Chain, the schools and how Keene's Pointe compares with the town's other gates, is in our Windermere guide.

Homes & Pricing

Where the money actually goes.

The housing stock is custom, built out mostly through the 2000s and 2010s with newer builds still appearing on remaining lots. What you pay is driven less by square footage than by position: interior street, golf frontage, interior lake, or the Butler Chain itself.

As of mid 2026, the community median has sat near $2.1 million. The table is the honest shorthand; the week to week picture is a phone call.

Who It Fits

The right buyer, honestly.

Keene's Pointe fits families who want the gate, the golf and the lakes without stepping all the way up to Isleworth pricing, and who will actually use what they are paying for: the club if they join, the water and the trails either way. It also fits buyers who care about resale, because a thousand homes behind one respected gate produce steady, readable comps.

It is the wrong answer for buyers who bristle at HOA structure or want to walk to dinner. For town center energy and new construction, Hamlin is fifteen minutes away and a different world. We show both without a preferred outcome; the commission is the same and the point is that you end up in the right one.

The Insider View

What we'd tell a buyer.

  1. Pick the village before the house

    The fourteen villages differ in lot size, age, architecture and price band, and two similar houses in different villages can behave very differently at resale. We rank villages against your budget and habits before we tour a single home.

  2. Decide the club question early

    Membership at The Golden Bear Club is a separate decision from the purchase, and social life here does divide between club families and everyone else. Structures and fees change, so we confirm the current picture with the club before you write an offer, not after.

  3. Read the water carefully

    Butler Chain frontage, interior lake frontage and lake access are three different assets at three different prices. Skiable water with a dock is the blue chip; a view across a pond is pleasant but should not be priced like the Chain.

  4. Budget the whole carry

    HOA dues, possible club costs, insurance on larger homes and the upkeep a community like this expects all belong in the math before you fall in love. We put the full monthly picture on one page early, because that is what we would want shown to us.

Keene's Pointe Questions

What buyers ask us.

How much do homes here cost in 2026?

As of mid 2026, interior homes typically start around $1.3 million, golf and interior lake positions run roughly $2.5 million to $6.5 million, and Butler Chain lakefront estates run from about $3.5 million to $7.5 million and beyond. The community median has recently sat near $2.1 million, though it moves with the mix of what happens to sell.

Do you have to join the Golden Bear Club?

No. Membership is a separate decision, and plenty of residents live happily without it. Social life does tend to divide between club families and everyone else, and membership structures and fees change, so we confirm the current picture with the club before our clients write an offer.

Is there Butler Chain access?

Yes, beyond the lakefront estates themselves the community includes lake access for residents, which is a meaningful part of the value here. The difference between direct frontage, deeded access and a view is large, and it shows up in resale.

What are the fourteen villages?

Each village has its own character, lot sizes and price band, from established custom streets to golf frontage and lakefront. Which one fits you depends on budget, whether golf or water matters more, and how close to the club you want to be. That is a conversation, not a chart, and we are glad to have it.

Keene's Pointe or Isleworth?

Keene's Pointe is larger, more family oriented, and starts lower. Isleworth is smaller, more private and more expensive. We live in Windermere and are glad to walk both with you.

Living in Lakes of Windermere

Lakes of Windermere sits in the 34786 zip in southwest Windermere, between the town and Horizon West.

It is platted in several sections. Most of the homes are single family, from about 1,700 to 3,750 square feet; the Lake Reams townhomes run about 1,200 to 1,550 square feet; and Peachtree is a gated section of single family homes.

Why buyers pick it: a Windermere address and the Windermere High zone at prices well below the gated golf communities, mature landscaping, and no CDD according to the MLS records. The tradeoff is age. Many homes are 15 to 20 years old, so roofs and systems are the conversation.

How it compares with Isleworth, Keene’s Pointe and the rest of the town is in our Windermere guide.

Lakes of Windermere HOA and CDD

Short answer: HOA yes, and no CDD. The MLS shows “CDD: No” on every closed sale here in the last twelve months.

  1. HOA dues by section

    On this year’s single family sales, MLS lists master association dues of about $225 to $245 a quarter. The gated Peachtree section lists about $510 to $530 a quarter, and the Lake Reams townhomes about $200 a month. Amenities listed on MLS include community pools, tennis courts, playgrounds and parks.

  2. No Community Development District

    All 41 closed sales in the last twelve months are recorded in Stellar MLS with no CDD. We confirm it on the Orange County tax bill before you write an offer.

  3. Older homes, real inspections

    With homes built from 2002 on, roof age and the insurance quote matter as much as the price. We get both before you waive anything.

Schools and drive times

The 2026 MLS listings here show Sunset Park Elementary, Horizon West Middle and Windermere High. Some listings in the Peachtree section show Bay Lake Elementary, so zones may differ by section. Buying or selling in Lakes of Windermere

What we tell buyers before they tour, and sellers before they list.

  1. Price the roof and the insurance

    A 2004 home with its original roof is a different purchase from a 2012 home with a new one. Get the insurance quote before the inspection period ends.

  2. Know which section you are in

    Dues, the gate and even the elementary school can change by section. We confirm all three on every address.

  3. Sellers: it still moves fast

    At 28 median days on market, Lakes of Windermere is quicker than the 34786 zip as a whole. Priced right, it goes under contract in under a month.

Lakes of Windermere questions

Still wondering about something? Ask us directly or call (813) 992-3073.

How much do homes in Lakes of Windermere cost in 2026?

Over the twelve months to October 2026, the median closed sale was $540,000 on 41 sales. The Lake Reams townhomes sold between $302,500 and $392,000 with a median of $353,000, and single family homes sold between $460,000 and $1.95 million with a median of $617,500 (Stellar MLS).

Does Lakes of Windermere have a CDD?

Not according to the MLS records. All 41 closed sales in the last twelve months are recorded in Stellar MLS with no Community Development District. We confirm it on the Orange County tax bill for any home you write an offer on.

What are the HOA fees in Lakes of Windermere?

On this year’s single family sales, MLS lists about $225 to $245 a quarter for the master association. The gated Peachtree section lists about $510 to $530 a quarter, and the Lake Reams townhomes about $200 a month.

Is Lakes of Windermere gated?

Most of it is not. Peachtree is the gated section, and it carries higher dues.

What schools serve Lakes of Windermere?

The 2026 MLS listings show Sunset Park Elementary, Horizon West Middle and Windermere High, with some Peachtree listings showing Bay Lake Elementary. Confirm the exact address with the OCPS school locator.

Living in Windermere Trails

Windermere Trails sits on the Reams Road side of Windermere in the 34786 zip, next to Horizon West, with Bay Lake Elementary at 12005 Silverlake Park Drive inside the community.

Meritage Homes is the builder named on the MLS listings. Almost every sale is single family, from about 1,800 to 4,300 square feet.

Why buyers pick it: a walk to the elementary school, a Windermere address, low dues and no CDD according to the MLS records, with the Disney side of the county close by. The tradeoff is pace. Homes here took longer to sell this year than most of the area.

How it compares with the rest of the town is in our Windermere guide, and the villages next door are in our Horizon West guide.

Windermere Trails HOA and CDD

Short answer: HOA yes, and no CDD. The MLS shows “CDD: No” on every closed sale here in the last twelve months.

  1. Low HOA dues

    Most of this year’s sales list about $244 a quarter, roughly $81 a month, on MLS. Amenities listed on MLS include the community pool, playgrounds, a dog park and parks.

  2. No Community Development District

    All 28 closed sales in the last twelve months are recorded in Stellar MLS with no CDD. We confirm it on the Orange County tax bill before you write an offer.

  3. A school inside the neighborhood

    Bay Lake Elementary sits inside the community, which is the reason many families shop here first.

Buying or selling in Windermere Trails

What we tell buyers before they tour, and sellers before they list.

  1. Buyers: time is on your side

    At 59 median days on market, sellers here are more open to credits and buydowns than in most of the area. Ask for them.

  2. Walking distance to school

    If walking to Bay Lake Elementary matters to you, tell us early. We will map which homes are an easy walk before you tour.

  3. Sellers: price for this pace

    Homes here took about two months to sell this year. Pricing right from day one, with a credit ready, beats a price cut in week six.

Windermere Trails questions

Still wondering about something? Ask us directly or call (813) 992-3073.

How much do homes in Windermere Trails cost in 2026?

Over the twelve months to October 2026, the median closed sale was $622,000 on 28 sales. Single family homes sold between $463,500 and $1,045,000 with a median of $625,000 (Stellar MLS).

Does Windermere Trails have a CDD?

Not according to the MLS records. All 28 closed sales in the last twelve months are recorded in Stellar MLS with no Community Development District. We confirm it on the Orange County tax bill for any home you write an offer on.

What are the HOA fees in Windermere Trails?

Most of this year’s sales list about $244 a quarter on MLS, roughly $81 a month, for the community pool, playgrounds, dog park and parks.

Who built Windermere Trails?

Meritage Homes is the builder named on the MLS listings, and the homes sold this year were built between 2012 and 2017.

Is there a school in Windermere Trails?

Yes. Bay Lake Elementary is inside the community at 12005 Silverlake Park Drive. The 2026 MLS listings show Horizon West Middle and Windermere High for middle and high school. Confirm the exact address with the OCPS school locator.

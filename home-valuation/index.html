Questions

Fair questions, direct answers.

How is this different from a Zillow Zestimate?

A Zestimate is an algorithm that has never seen your house. We pull the actual closed comparables on and around your street, adjust for your home's condition, upgrades and lot, and explain the number line by line. When we miss, it is usually by less than the algorithm's published error range for this zip code.

Is the valuation really free? What's the catch?

It is free and there is no obligation. We build valuations because some owners who receive one eventually decide to sell, and some of those choose us. Most of our business arrives by referral, and this is how referrals start.

How fast will I get my valuation?

Within 24 hours for homes in Windermere, Winter Garden, Horizon West and nearby West Orlando. Complex properties like lakefront, acreage, or significant renovations can take a little longer because we verify more.

Do I have to list my home with you afterward?

No. The valuation is yours to keep whether you list with us, list with someone else, or stay put for another decade.

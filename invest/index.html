Investors

Invest in Central Florida, with investors.

We buy, renovate, flip and hold in West Orlando with our own money. When we underwrite a deal for you, it is the same spreadsheet, the same contractors and the same conservative numbers we use on our own.

Fix and flip · Long term rentals · Off market deal flow · Windermere, Winter Garden, Horizon West, Clermont and beyond

The Short Version

Most agents will send you MLS listings and call it investor service. We run a fix and flip company and a rental portfolio in this market, which means we know what a 1990s Clermont roof costs to replace, what a Horizon West townhome actually rents for, and which wholesalers' numbers to believe.

What that gets you: a market value and an after repair value we will defend, a renovation budget from contractors who have done the work for us, an offer price with a margin built in, and an honest recommendation, including walk away. We say no to more deals than we say yes to, on our own account and on yours.

Who it fits: first flip or fiftieth, local or out of state, buy and hold or buy and sell. Who it does not fit: anyone looking for a guaranteed return or a reason to overpay. We are licensed Realtors, not financial advisors, and every deal carries risk that no spreadsheet removes.

What We Do

Four ways we work with investors.

Pick the one that matches where you are. Most clients start with the analysis and end up using all four.

  1. Fix and flip analysis

    Market value today, after repair value from closed comps within the last 90 days, a line item renovation estimate, holding and selling costs, and a maximum offer price that still leaves a margin. We run it on wholesale assignments, MLS listings, auctions and off market leads, and we tell you when the wholesaler's ARV is fiction.

  2. Long term rental underwriting

    Realistic rent from what is actually leasing on the street, not the listing's asking rent. Insurance quotes before you go under contract, because Florida premiums decide more deals than purchase price now. HOA and CDD on the tax bill. Vacancy, maintenance and management built in, so the cash flow number is the one you will see.

  3. Deal flow

    We see what crosses our own desk: wholesaler assignments, estate and pre foreclosure situations from our seller side, stale listings where a seller will finance, and builder closeouts in Horizon West. When something fits your criteria it goes to you with the numbers already run, usually the same day.

  4. The bench

    Contractors, roofers, pool companies, inspectors, insurance brokers, title and hard money lenders we have paid ourselves. For out of state investors we walk the property, send video, and manage the inspection and the walk through so you do not have to fly in for every step.

How We Underwrite

Conservative numbers, on purpose.

After repair value from closed sales, never active listings. Renovation budgets with a contingency, because every house has one surprise. Rents from what leased, not what was asked. Insurance quoted before the offer. Holding costs at the real rate, which is 7.4 percent or a hard money rate, not the one from two years ago. If the deal only works with optimistic inputs, it does not work.

Investor Questions

What investors ask us.

Is Central Florida a good place to invest in real estate in 2026?

It is a good place to invest carefully. The Orlando metro is at 5.3 months of supply with 66 days on market and 7.4 percent mortgage rates, which means more negotiating room on the buy side than any time since 2019, and a slower exit on the sell side. Population and job growth are still strong, especially around Disney, the hospitals and the 429 corridor. Deals that are underwritten on today's rates and real rents work. Deals built on 2021 assumptions do not.

Do you work with out of state investors?

Yes, and a lot of our investor clients have never been to the property before closing. We walk it, send video, coordinate the inspection, manage the contractor walk through and attend the closing. You see everything we see, with our notes on what it means.

How do you find off market deals?

From our own seller side: estates, relocations and pre foreclosure situations where a seller wants a clean cash close. From wholesalers whose assignments we have bought and whose numbers we have checked. From stale listings where the owner will consider seller financing. And from builders in Horizon West clearing closeouts. We send what fits your criteria with the numbers already run.

What about short term rentals near Disney?

Most of Windermere, Winter Garden and Horizon West does not allow short term rentals under HOA rules or county zoning, and buying there for Airbnb is the most common investor mistake we see. The zoned short term rental communities are mostly in the Kissimmee and Davenport corridor, with their own fee structure and heavy competition. We will tell you which side of that line a property sits on before you offer.

Do you charge investors more?

No. On a purchase we are typically paid through the transaction like any buyer's agent, and the analysis is part of the job. On a flip we can list the finished home, which is where our own flips go. There is no fee for looking at a deal, and the honest answer on most of them is no.

The Process

From shortlist to keys.

  1. Define the target

    Schools, commute, lifestyle, budget, resale. We build a shortlist of neighborhoods that actually fit, and tell you which popular ones do not fit you and why.

  2. See it with an investor's eye

    Every showing comes with an honest read on condition, likely repair costs and resale strength. We have talked clients out of more houses than we can count.

  3. Win the negotiation

    Offer strategy built from closed comps and the seller's actual position, not list price. In multiple offer situations we structure terms that win without overpaying.

  4. Protect the contract

    Inspection, appraisal, financing deadlines and repair negotiation, managed daily. Florida contracts punish missed dates. Ours do not get missed.

  5. Close and stay

    We attend your closing, hand you the keys, and stay your first call for contractors, insurance questions and, when the time comes, the sale.

Buyer Questions

Asked on every first call.

Do I pay my buyer's agent in Florida?

Since the 2024 industry changes, buyer agent compensation is agreed in writing before touring. In many transactions the seller still contributes, but it is negotiated deal by deal. We put our terms in plain language up front, so you know exactly what our representation costs and who pays it before you ever walk a house.

Should I use the builder's agent for new construction?

The builder's sales agent works for the builder. Your representation typically costs you nothing extra, and we negotiate upgrades, closing cost credits and contract terms the on-site agent will not volunteer. We walk every construction phase and attend the final inspection with you.

How competitive is the market right now?

As of mid 2026 the market is balanced. Windermere homes are going pending in about 29 days and Winter Garden averages about 48 days on market. Well priced homes still draw multiple offers, but buyers have real negotiating room on homes that sat. Strategy depends on the specific house, which is exactly what we advise on.

Do you work with relocating buyers?

Yes. A large share of our buyers relocate to Central Florida from other states and countries. We run video tours, build neighborhood shortlists around schools and commutes, and serve clients in English, Russian and Ukrainian.

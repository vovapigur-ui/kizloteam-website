The Process

How we take a home to market.

  1. The honest valuation

    We pull closed comps ourselves, walk your home, and give you a number backed by evidence, together with a written net sheet showing what you keep after every cost.

  2. Preparation and staging

    Anastasiia builds the presentation plan: what to repair, what to remove, how each room photographs. Most sellers spend far less than they expect here and earn it back multiplied.

  3. Photography and launch

    Professional photography, listing copy written for your buyer, and a coordinated launch across the MLS, Zillow, our Google presence and our own network before the first showing.

  4. Showings and feedback

    You get a weekly written update with showing counts, buyer feedback and market movement. If something needs adjusting, you hear it from us first, with a recommendation.

  5. Negotiation and closing

    Offers, inspection requests and appraisal issues are negotiated from evidence, not emotion. We manage every deadline through closing day and stay reachable after it.

Seller Words
Seller Questions

Asked before every listing.

How much does it cost to sell a house here?

Plan for commission, which is negotiable and agreed in writing before listing, plus title and closing costs, prorated taxes, and any repairs negotiated after inspection. On most West Orlando sales, total costs land between 6 and 8 percent of the sale price. Every seller gets a written net sheet before committing, so the number at closing is never a surprise.

What is my home actually worth?

Online estimates in Windermere and Winter Garden routinely miss by 5 to 10 percent because they cannot see condition, upgrades or lot position. We prepare a valuation from closed comparable sales we pull ourselves, adjusted for your specific home, at no cost and with no obligation to list.

How long will it take to sell?

As of mid 2026, well prepared Windermere homes are going pending in roughly 29 days, and the Winter Garden average is about 48 days on market. Preparation and pricing decide which side of the average you land on.

Do I need to stage my home?

Not always fully, but presentation is not optional in this market. Anastasiia walks every listing before photos and tells you exactly what to move, remove or refresh. Most of it costs little or nothing and shows up directly in the offers.

What if my home needs work first?

Vlad renovates property in this market as an investor, so we can price the repairs honestly, bring in contractors we actually use, and tell you which projects return money at closing and which ones do not.

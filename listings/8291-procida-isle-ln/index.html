The Short Version

A single story four bedroom on a quiet street in Windermere Isle, with a heated saltwater pool put in new in 2021 and a nine kilowatt solar array on the roof. Every one of the fourteen county permits on this address is closed.

The house was built in 2019 and has had one owner. What that owner added since is the part worth paying attention to: the pool and paver deck, the gas line that feeds both the pool heater and the grill stub, the solar, the water softener and filtration, and the EV charger in the garage. All of it permitted, all of it finaled, all of it done by licensed contractors.

Everything lives on one floor. The primary suite is downstairs with the other three bedrooms, and the laundry is on the same level with a sink and the washer and dryer staying with the house.

What Sets It Apart

Four things you cannot add later.

Most homes at this price in Windermere Isle are the same floor plan. These are the differences that took permits, contractors and time.

  1. A pool that was done properly

    Inground concrete, built by Dreamscapes Pools and Spas and finaled in February 2021. Gas heated off a dedicated 400,000 BTU line, saltwater, with a colour changing light. Natural stone in the back and a pergola for shade. Maintenance runs about $150 a month.

  2. Nine kilowatts on the roof

    Installed by Suntria in 2021 on a permitted value of $39,000. The system is financed, and the loan is paid off at closing from the seller's proceeds, so it conveys to you free and clear.

  3. A permit file with nothing open

    Orange County shows fourteen permits on this address. All fourteen are complete and finaled with passed inspections. No open items, no expired items, nothing unpermitted. That is rarer than it should be, and it is the difference between a clean inspection period and a negotiation.

  4. The systems are already handled

    Roof and air conditioning are both original to the 2019 build. Water softener and whole house filtration are in. Smart thermostat, doorbell, locks and garage opener are installed and stay. So does the EV charger in the garage.

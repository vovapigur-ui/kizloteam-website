# Accessibility

**Last reviewed: October 20, 2026**

The Kizlo Team is committed to making kizloteam.com usable by everyone, including people who browse with a screen reader, a keyboard, screen magnification, or other assistive technology.

## Our standard

We work toward conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. We review the Site against that standard when we make changes, and we treat anything that falls short of it as a defect to be fixed rather than a limitation to be documented.

## Where we know we fall short

Some pages set text over a photograph, and the home page sets it over a background video, so contrast depends on the picture behind the words. We use a darkening scrim to keep that text legible, but we cannot guarantee a fixed contrast ratio on every photograph and every video frame at every screen size. The video has a pause button, and it does not play when your device asks for reduced motion or to save data. If any of it is hard for you to read, tell us and we will change the image or the treatment.

## Tell us if something does not work

If any page, form, or document on this Site is difficult to use, we want to hear about it, and we will help you get the same information another way. There is no wrong way to describe the problem.

## Assistance with real estate documents

Real estate transactions involve long documents from third parties, including lenders, title companies, and inspectors, whose formats we do not control. If you need a document in a different format or read aloud to you, ask and we will arrange it.

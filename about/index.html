How we work with you

Four habits that hold up on every deal, whether you are buying your first home or selling on the water.

  1. Tell the truth first

    You will hear the honest number, the honest condition and the honest timeline before you hear anything else. Clients stay with us because the advice holds up after closing.

  2. Prepare before exposure

    Pricing, staging, photography and pre-inspection issues get handled before a listing goes live. The first two weeks on market decide the outcome, so we never waste them.

  3. Negotiate from evidence

    Every position we take is backed by closed comps, inspection data or contractor pricing. Emotional negotiation loses to prepared negotiation, quietly, every time.

  4. Answer the phone

    Responsiveness comes up in review after review. Deals in this market move in hours, not days, and so do we.

The Story

We built this team the way we tell clients to buy a house: carefully.

Vlad came to real estate from the investment side. He analyzes deals, renovates and holds property in this market with his own money, and he brings that same discipline to every client transaction. If a house has a problem, he will find it. If a price is wrong, he will say so, even when it costs him the sale.

Anastasiia is the reason Kizlo Team listings photograph the way they do. She handles staging, preparation and presentation, and she has an unreasonable attention to detail about all three. Sellers see the difference in their photos. Buyers feel it when they walk in.

We are licensed Realtors with Keller Williams Realty At The Lakes. We serve clients in English, Russian and Ukrainian, and we raise our son a short drive from most of the homes we sell. This is not a territory on a map for us. It is home.

How We Work

The approach

  1. Tell the truth first

    You will hear the honest number, the honest condition and the honest timeline before you hear anything else. Clients stay with us because the advice holds up after closing.

  2. Prepare before exposure

    Pricing, staging, photography and pre-inspection issues get handled before a listing goes live. The first ten days on market decide the outcome, so we never waste them.

  3. Negotiate from evidence

    Every position we take is backed by closed comps, inspection data or contractor pricing. Emotional negotiation loses to prepared negotiation, quietly, every time.

  4. Answer the phone

    Responsiveness is the most repeated word in our reviews. Deals in this market move in hours, not days, and so do we.
